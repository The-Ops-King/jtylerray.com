Privacy

Last updated 12 September 2026

This site is run by J. Tyler Ray. It is a small site with no accounts, no cookies and no analytics, so this is short.

01 What I collect

One thing: whatever you type into the contact form on the contact card. That form asks for your name, a message, and either an email address or a phone number so there is a way to answer you. Nothing else on this site collects anything about you.

The form also carries a hidden field that real people never see or fill in. It exists to catch automated submissions, and anything entered in it is discarded.

02 What happens to it

It is sent to me as an email, through an email service called Resend, and it lands in my inbox at jt@jtylerray.com. There is no database behind this site and no list you are added to. I use what you send to reply to you and, if we end up working together, to do the work.

I do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

03 What the servers see

The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs: IP address, browser, the page requested, the time. That is how any web server works, and it is used for running the site and keeping it secure.

The typefaces are served by Google Fonts, so loading a page makes a request to Google, which sees your IP address as part of it.

04 Cookies and tracking

There are none. No analytics, no advertising pixels, no cross-site tracking, and no cookies set by this site.

One exception worth naming: the design lab at /brand-1, which is a working page rather than part of the site proper, remembers your display choices in your own browser's local storage. That stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

05 Other companies involved

  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps the server logs above.
  • Resend delivers the contact form to my inbox.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces.
  • Google Calendar handles booking, but only if you choose to book a call.

Each of them has its own privacy policy covering what it does with that.

06 How long I keep it

Enquiries stay in my email for as long as they are useful: while we are talking, and afterwards as a record of the work. If you want yours deleted, ask and I will delete it.

07 Asking about your information

Email jt@jtylerray.com and I will tell you what I hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Depending on where you live you may have a formal right to those things. You do not need to invoke it. Just ask.

08 Children

This site is for people running businesses. It is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.

09 Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes here with a new date at the top.

Questions about any of this: jt@jtylerray.com. See also the terms.
